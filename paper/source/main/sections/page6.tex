\subsection{Correct relations do not exhaust a world}
All three N2 obstruction worlds satisfy every fixed query.
Two are Drayton Hall A/B; the third is Fig.~\ref{fig:conflict}.
These cases distinguish fixed-query coverage from consistency of the
submitted world. A model can realize the queried relations and create
a conflict in additional material.
Registered and all-declared scopes make the distinction visible.

N2 joint registered success (Eq.~\ref{eq:joint}) is 42/96 for A and
49/96 for B; joint all-declared success is 14/96 and 13/96.
For CS1, the corresponding counts are 1/16 versus 3/16, and 1/16
versus 1/16. No PASS certifies the complete real building.

\subsection{Local level changes and assemblies}
Lovell Q04 involves a two-riser UP transition rather than an ordinary
story change. GPT R succeeds 0/8, A 2/8, and B 5/8.
Gamble's ordinary floor distinction in Q01 instead yields 8/8, 8/8, and 7/8.
The contrast locates a difficulty in this local case; it does not
show a general failure to identify floors.

Assemblies have obligations beyond labels: parent and part materials
must agree, landings must support endpoints, and space entities cannot
silently replace landing interfaces. These checks distinguish an
unrealized connection from a plausible description.

\subsection{Instrument errors remain distinct}
A later slab-opening diagnostic found a 2.2.1 limitation.
In a predeclared ten-world cohort, eight re-encodings preserved final
material but changed a detached-opening violation label; two changed
material and do not establish an error.
The eight became unevaluable, not PASS. Fixed readouts and registered
verdicts were unchanged, and historical A/B declared-PASS counts did
not increase. We preserve the primary version and disclose this
diagnostic rather than repairing selected outputs.
