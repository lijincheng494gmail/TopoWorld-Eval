# Drawing sources and redistribution basis

Checked 5 November 2026. The 42 images are measured-drawing sheets from 14 HABS records held by the Library of Congress (LOC), Prints & Photographs Division. Private Loft photographs are excluded.

LOC’s [HABS/HAER/HALS rights guidance](https://wwws.loc.gov/rr/print/res/114_habs.html) identifies original measured drawings as U.S. Government work in the public domain. Each linked item retains an exception for images copied from third parties. The selected resources are measured sheets. This package asserts no new license over archival material.

Credit: Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS). Survey identifiers and individual sheet URLs are below; file hashes and source titles are in `data/IMAGE_MANIFEST.json`.
